Supplies

Basic Info

To start, there are plenty of things you may need, and plenty of things you may already have lying around!

The most important thing about garage kits is your own safety! Use an N-95 mask when prepping your kits, and use a half-mask with two cartridges when you're painting with lacquer paints!

Prep & Assembly Supplies

For prepping, the most basic items you'll need are wire cutters, sandpaper, a hobby or X-acto knife, drill/hand drill, dill bits, wires/pins, putty, and a resin wash.

For assembly, you will need super glue.
